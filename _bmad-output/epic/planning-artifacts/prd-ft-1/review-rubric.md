# PRD Quality Review — Pomodoro tracker (FT-1 / FT-3)

Reviewed: `prd.md` and `addendum.md` in this folder, against `.claude/skills/bmad-prd/assets/prd-validation-checklist.md`.
Calibration: hobby/solo learning project, single user, no launch — but chain-top (feeds UX, architecture, decomposition of FT-1 into Stories). Done-ness and downstream usability are weighted up; persona depth and market rigor are weighted down. This is an unreviewed Fast-path draft, so `[ASSUMPTION]` density is expected and is judged on whether the right things were surfaced.

## Overall verdict

This is a good chain-top draft. It has a real thesis ("you cannot miss its end" plus "the record is never wrong", with the Tree as the primary statistic), product-specific NFRs with numbers, 26 contiguous FRs most of which carry testable consequences, a glossary that is actually used, and honest scope boundaries. What is at risk is not any single section but one decision and a handful of cross-FR seams: Open Question 1 (automatic start of the next Work Interval) is framed as a UX blocker, but it silently changes the shape of UJ-2, FR-6, FR-15, SM-1 and SM-2; and a few FR pairs (FR-19 vs FR-18, FR-4 vs FR-18, FR-3 vs the skip-Break assumption in FR-5) contradict each other in ways story decomposition would trip on. Resolve OQ1 and the two high findings in author review and this PRD is ready to feed UX and architecture.

## Decision-readiness — adequate

The PRD states decisions as decisions: server-side storage without accounts, Tasks never deleted, automatic work→break transition, Interrupted Pomodoros recorded but not counted. The one genuine tension is named where it lives — the `[NOTE FOR PM]` under FR-5 ("the one place where the brief's vision and the 'record is never wrong' goal pull apart") is exactly the callout the rubric asks for, and it names what each option gives up.

Where it falls short is in how far that tension is allowed to spread. OQ1 is labelled "needs a decision before UX work", but it is a requirements decision, not a UX one: under auto-start, UJ-2 ("His record for the day is correct without any manual fix") cannot be true as written — a Pomodoro auto-started at 14:30 would be running unattended at 14:50 and would complete as a phantom record at 14:55. The UJ dodges this by ending on "what state the timer is in now" without saying which state. SM-1 measures only the missing direction ("every Pomodoro the author finished appears"), so the PRD's own "record is never wrong" claim currently has no metric for the spurious direction. The PRD is honest about the fork but has not traced its consequences.

Three of the five Open Questions (OQ2, OQ3, OQ5) are "confirm X" where X is already assumed inline and indexed. That is fine for a draft awaiting author review, but they are confirmations, not open questions; only OQ1 and OQ4 are genuinely undecided.

### Findings
- **high** OQ1 is a requirements fork, not a UX detail (§4.1 FR-5, §2.3 UJ-2, §9 SM-1/SM-2, §10 OQ1) — The auto-start assumption in FR-5 conflicts with UJ-2's "record for the day is correct without any manual fix" and with §4.4's "trustworthy" statistics; SM-1 only checks for missing records, never for phantom Completed records produced by an unattended auto-start. *Fix:* Decide OQ1 in author review before UX starts. If auto-start is kept, add a consequence to FR-5 bounding it (e.g. auto-start only if the user acted on the Break-end Alert; otherwise the timer goes idle and FR-15 shows the summary) and add a phantom-record check to SM-1 ("and no Completed Pomodoro Record exists for an interval the author was not working"). If auto-start is dropped, rewrite FR-5's assumption as a decision and delete the SM-C1 note about guarding OQ1.
- **low** OQ2, OQ3, OQ5 are confirmations of already-taken assumptions (§10) — Each has its answer stated inline and indexed in §11. *Fix:* Move them into the Assumptions Index as "confirm during author review" items and leave §10 with the two questions that are actually open (OQ1, OQ4), so a downstream reader sees the real decision load at a glance.

## Substance over theater — strong

No furniture worth cutting. The single persona is the author and it drives every decision (no auth, macOS-only, Chrome first, keyboard operability instead of an accessibility standard). The three UJs each produce FRs (UJ-2 alone motivates FR-6, FR-15, FR-17). The Vision (§1) could not be swapped into another Pomodoro PRD — "the remaining time stays visible outside the app tab" and "each Task has its own Tree" are this product. NFRs (§5) carry thresholds (1 s, 2 s) rather than adjectives. There is no differentiation section, which is correct: the addendum's research digest does the landscape work and stays out of the requirements narrative. §2.1 JTBD is the only section that reads as template; it is five lines and harmless.

## Strategic coherence — strong

The thesis is explicit and the PRD says so: §4.3's "This feature is the reason the product exists." The features serve a single arc — see it running, cannot miss its end, the record survives, the record becomes a Tree. MVP scope kind is problem-solving and the scope logic matches (§8.2 defers by "no v1 benefit", "low value for one user", "until a month of real data exists" — reasons, not just a list). SM-1 and SM-2 validate the thesis directly rather than counting activity; SM-3 is the honest replacement test; counter-metrics exist and SM-C1 is a real guard, not decoration.

One gap: SM-2's measurement is specified in a way the product as written cannot produce.

### Findings
- **medium** SM-2 is not measurable from the product as specified (§9 SM-2, §5 Privacy, §3 Pomodoro Record, §4.3 FR-15) — SM-2 measures "the time between the Interval end and the next Start, Reset, or summary dismissal", but the Pomodoro Record stores no acknowledgement time, §5 forbids telemetry, FR-15 suppresses the summary when the end was seen in the foreground (so there is nothing to dismiss), and under the FR-5 auto-start assumption there is no "next Start" after a Break. The "except when the author was deliberately away" clause also makes the target unfalsifiable. *Fix:* Either make SM-2 a manual 7-day diary metric like SM-1 (author notes each missed end), or add an "acknowledged at" timestamp to the Pomodoro Record (Glossary + FR-16) and define "acted on" as the first user action in the app after the end. Replace "deliberately away" with a bound (e.g. exclude Days the author marks as away).
- **low** No build or drop order among the seven features (§8.1, §6 "Learning-project scope") — Everything is in scope with equal weight; §6 says scope growth "should be resisted" but nothing says what gives if it must. The addendum notes Persistence is a dependency for Tree and Statistics, which is the only ordering hint. *Fix:* One sentence in §8 naming the core (Timer, Alerts, Persistence) and what would be cut first (Week view, Garden) if the learning-project timebox bites. Cheap, and decomposition can use it directly.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate

Most FRs would pass an engineer's "what does done look like" test: FR-6 (within 1 second, never negative, closed-app rule), FR-7 (100 chars, trimmed, duplicate rule), FR-11 (per-second foreground, per-minute background, never more than one minute stale), FR-13 (request on first Start, never re-ask after denial, retry button), FR-24 (ranges in whole minutes, applies to the next Interval). That is a high floor for a Fast-path draft.

The problems are at the seams between FRs rather than inside them, which is exactly where story decomposition will split them apart and lose the context.

### Findings
- **high** FR-19 contradicts FR-18 on offline Completed Pomodoros (§4.5 FR-19, §4.4 FR-18, §2.3 UJ-1) — FR-19 says the Tree "never depends on state that exists only in the browser", but FR-18 says a Completed record is "kept in the browser and submitted when the server is reachable again". Read literally, a Pomodoro completed while the server is down does not grow the Tree until sync, which breaks UJ-1's "the Tree ... is one step taller" and makes FR-21's "update as soon as a Pomodoro is Completed" untrue in the same case. Architecture will have to pick one. *Fix:* Rewrite FR-19's third consequence as "the Tree equals the number of Completed Pomodoro Records, including records queued for submission under FR-18; after sync it equals the server's count" — or state that the Tree lags while unsaved records exist and the FR-18 indicator covers it.
- **medium** Skip Break is a requirement hidden in an assumption tag (§4.1 FR-5 assumption, FR-3) — "The user can also skip a running Break and start the next Pomodoro immediately" is a user-facing capability with no FR and no consequence; a story writer filtering for FRs will miss it. It also overlaps FR-3's "Resetting a Break ends the Break; the timer returns to idle" with a different outcome (idle vs. next Pomodoro started). *Fix:* Add a consequence to FR-5 ("The user can end a running Break early; the next Work Interval starts on the Current Task") and say in FR-3 that Reset on a Break and Skip are the same action or different ones.
- **medium** The Garden is in MVP scope but has no requirement (§8.1, §3 Glossary, §4.2 FR-10, §4.5 FR-20) — The Garden appears in the Glossary, in FR-10 and FR-20 as a destination, and in §8.1 as delivered scope, but no FR says what it shows (Trees, Completed counts, Done date?), how it is reached, or how it is ordered. UX has one clause to design from and a "Garden" story would have no acceptance criteria. *Fix:* Add FR-27 under §4.5 with two or three consequences, or explicitly fold the Garden into FR-20 as a Task-list filter and drop the separate noun.
- **medium** FR-15's trigger condition is undefined (§4.3 FR-15) — "Interval ends they did not see" and "not shown when the user saw the Interval end in the foreground" have no observable definition of "saw". This drives both UX (when does the summary appear) and SM-2. *Fix:* Define it once: "the Interval end counts as seen if the app tab was visible and the window focused at the moment of the end, or the user acted in the app within N seconds after it."
- **medium** FR-4 ordering constraint conflicts with FR-18 (§4.1 FR-4, §4.4 FR-16/FR-18) — "The Pomodoro Record is stored with outcome Completed before the Break is shown as running" reads as a server round-trip blocking the UI, which FR-18 forbids ("starting the next Interval is not blocked"). *Fix:* "recorded (locally at minimum, per FR-18) before the Break is shown as running; submitted to the server per FR-16."
- **medium** Concurrent tabs or browsers are not addressed anywhere (§4.3, §4.4 FR-16, §4.1 FR-17) — FR-16 explicitly invites "the app is opened in another browser on the same machine", and FR-17 restores a running Pomodoro on reopen, so two live instances are a normal case. Nothing says whether both fire Alerts (violating SM-C2's "one notification and one sound"), which one owns the countdown, or whether a second tab can Start. *Fix:* One assumption or constraint in §6: "One instance controls the timer; additional tabs show the same state and do not issue Alerts" — or a `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` declaring concurrent instances unsupported with a stated behaviour.
- **low** FR-25 has no consequences block (§4.7 FR-25) — The only FR without one. The behaviour is simple but the format break will propagate into a story with no acceptance criteria. *Fix:* Two lines: default on; when off, no sound plays and the notification and tab title Alerts are unaffected.
- **low** Marking a Task Done during a Break is unspecified (§4.2 FR-10, FR-1, FR-5) — FR-10 blocks Done only "with a running or paused Pomodoro"; a Break is an Interval but not a Pomodoro, so Done during a Break is allowed, and under FR-5 auto-start the next Pomodoro would then have no Current Task. FR-1 uses the broader "while an Interval is running". *Fix:* Align FR-10 to FR-1: "while any Interval is running or paused on it".
- **low** A Break that ends while the app is closed is not covered (§4.1 FR-6 third consequence) — FR-6 handles the Work Interval case ("stored as Completed ... timer opens idle") but says nothing about a Break whose scheduled end passed while closed. The intended outcome is almost certainly "opens idle", but it is inferred. *Fix:* Extend the clause: "If a Break's scheduled end passed, the timer opens idle."
- **low** FR-14 "must not fail silently" without a defined alternative (§4.3 FR-14) — This is the "handles X gracefully" pattern the rubric flags. *Fix:* Mirror FR-13: "if the sound cannot play, the app shows the same non-blocking indicator that sound is unavailable."

## Scope honesty — strong

Omissions are explicit and the Non-Goals (§7) do real work: no delete, no export, no menu-bar app, no gamification beyond the Tree — each of which a reader might otherwise assume. `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` callouts sit at the two places a builder would silently add scope (delete a Task in FR-10, configurable cadence in FR-24). §8.2 defers with reasons and the `[NOTE FOR PM]` on editing past records is a real "this will come back" flag. Fourteen assumptions, five OQs and three PM notes on an unreviewed hobby-PRD is the right density, and the assumptions are the right ones: each is a place where the brief was silent and the choice changes behaviour (Cycle reset per Day, closed-app Pomodoro counts as Completed, offline server counts as "unrecorded"). The one silent omission of consequence is concurrent instances, filed under Done-ness above.

## Downstream usability — adequate

This is where the chain-top weighting bites. The Glossary is present, the IDs are clean (FR-1 to FR-26 contiguous and unique; UJ-1 to UJ-3; SM-1 to SM-3, SM-C1, SM-C2), cross-references resolve, every UJ has "Denis" as protagonist, and the Assumptions Index round-trips (14 inline, 14 indexed). Each §4 feature block opens with a description that stands alone and names the UJs it realizes, so a feature can be pulled out into a Story without "see above". The addendum's "Pointers for decomposition" section is a useful hand-off.

The gaps are undefined UI nouns and an undefined timer state vocabulary. UX and story creation will each invent their own and diverge.

### Findings
- **low** Timer states are used but never defined (§3, §4.1 FR-3/FR-6, §4.3 FR-11) — "idle" (FR-3, FR-6, FR-11), "paused", "running", and an implicit "ended, not yet acknowledged" state (FR-11 fourth consequence, FR-15) form the state machine architecture will build, but none is a Glossary entry. *Fix:* Add a "Timer state" Glossary entry listing the states and the transitions between them in one line each; FR-11 and FR-15 then reference them by name.
- **low** "Task list" and "Task picker" are distinct and neither is defined (§2.3 UJ-1/UJ-3, §4.2 FR-8, §4.5 FR-20) — UJ-3 has the Task "disappear from the Task picker" while still appearing in "the Task list" with the tallest Tree; FR-8 says the picker "lists Open Tasks only"; FR-20 puts the Tree "next to every Task in the Task list" (every Task including Done?). Two surfaces with different contents, no Glossary entry for either. *Fix:* Add both to the Glossary, state which shows Done Tasks, and say whether the Task list is the same view as the Garden.

## Shape fit — strong

The PRD is shaped correctly for what it is: a hobby, single-operator, chain-top product with meaningful UX. Three UJs is the right count — enough to anchor the visibility thesis (UJ-1), the recovery thesis (UJ-2) and the statistics/Tree lifecycle (UJ-3), not so many that they become theater. SMs are user-facing but measured by the author's own tally, which is the honest option when telemetry is a non-goal. NFRs are five bullets with numbers rather than a compliance matrix. The addendum keeps research and mechanism notes out of the requirements narrative, which is the right split for a PRD that feeds architecture. Nothing is over- or under-formalized.

## Mechanical notes

- **Glossary drift — "Growth Step".** Defined as an increment ("one increment of a Tree's growth"), used as a level in FR-19 ("whose Growth Step equals the number of Completed Pomodoros") and FR-7/FR-10 ("initial Growth Step", "continues from its current Growth Step"). Pick one: either "Growth Step" is the increment and the Tree has a "Stage" or "Height" equal to the count, or "Growth Step" is the level and FR-19's first consequence reads "advances by one".
- **Glossary — Short Break / Long Break** are defined only inside the "Break" entry, not as headwords, but are used as terms in FR-4, FR-24 and §3 Settings. Promote them to headwords or bold them inside the Break entry.
- **Glossary — "Cycle" scope.** "The sequence of Completed Pomodoros since the last Long Break" does not say whether the Cycle spans Tasks or is per Task. All FRs read as if it is global; say so.
- **Cross-reference — FR-5** cites "The Alert for the Break end is issued (FR-12 to FR-14)". The Alert per Glossary includes the tab title change, which is FR-11; should be FR-11 to FR-14.
- **Bare assumption tag — §6 Cost.** "Zero recurring cost beyond the author's own machine. `[ASSUMPTION]`" has no inline text; the index (§11) supplies it. Put the text inline for round-trip consistency.
- **Assumptions Index roundtrip:** 14 inline, 14 indexed, all resolve. OK.
- **ID continuity:** FR-1..26, UJ-1..3, SM-1..3, SM-C1..C2 — contiguous, unique. OK.
- **UJ protagonists:** all three name Denis inline. OK.
- **Naming:** §4.3 is "Always-visible state and Alerts"; the addendum's decomposition pointer calls it "Alerts and visibility". Align so the Story title matches the PRD section.
- **Header:** "*Working title — confirm.*" is still under the H1; SM-2 "except when the author was deliberately away" is an unbounded exemption (see the SM-2 finding).
- **Required sections for stakes:** Vision, Target User with UJs, Glossary, Features/FRs, NFRs, Constraints, Non-Goals, MVP Scope, Success Metrics with counter-metrics, Open Questions, Assumptions Index — all present. Nothing missing for a hobby chain-top PRD.
